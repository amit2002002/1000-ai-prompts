Frequently Asked Questions

How many prompts are included? ▼
There are 1,000 prompt variations in total: one black-and-white prompt and one matching colored reference prompt for each design.
Can I copy the prompts? ▼
Yes. Each prompt is intended to be easy to select, copy, and customize for your workflow.
Which AI image generator should I use? ▼
You can try the prompts with your preferred AI image-generation tool. Results may differ between platforms.
Can I use the generated images in a coloring book? ▼
That depends on the image generator's terms, the originality of the output, and the rules of your intended publishing platform. Review licensing, content policies, and print quality before publishing.
Will every generated image look exactly the same? ▼
No. AI tools can interpret prompts differently. Treat the prompts as creative guidance and refine the generated results as needed.
